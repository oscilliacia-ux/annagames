Birthday Party "90s Cinema"

Note: This is a simulated portfolio project designed to demonstrate practical application of Project Management methodologies, tools, and risk assessment. No real clients or vendors were involved.

End-to-end organization of an adult 90s-themed birthday party for 30 guests: concept, budget, vendor management, event program, and day-of coordination.

🎯 The Challenge

The client wanted to celebrate a birthday party in a 90s style: creating an immersive atmosphere, music, decor, and a program, rather than a standard banquet.

Constraints: 6 weeks for preparation, a budget of 250,000 RUB, 30 guests, and a loft venue in the city center.

My task as PM: Assemble the concept, budget, vendors, and timeline; execute the event seamlessly and stay within budget.

✅ Objectives & Success Criteria

  • Execute the event on the scheduled date without postponements.
  • Stay within the 250,000 RUB budget (reserve fund — max 10%).
  • Finalize all vendor contracts at least 10 days before the event.
  • Provide a minimum 4-hour program without "dead air" between blocks.
  • Collect guest feedback and a photo report.

📦 Project Scope

✔ In-Scope

  • Concept creation and mood boarding
  • Venue selection and booking
  • Budget planning and cost control
  • Vendor management (Host, DJ, Photographer, Catering, Decor)
  • Guest list management and RSVP tracking
  • Event program and run-of-show (timing)
  • Setup, day-of coordination, and teardown

✘ Out-of-Scope

  • Guest transportation
  • Accommodation for out-of-town guests
  • Professional video recording
  • Individual guest favors

👥 Stakeholders & Team

  • Client (Sponsor) — The birthday person and their partner (Approved concept, budget, and guest list).
  • Guests — 30 adults.
  • Venue — Loft administrator.
  • Vendors — Host, DJ, Photographer, Caterer, Decorator.
  • Project Team — PM (me) and Assistant.

My Responsibilities: Budgeting, contract negotiations, briefings, timeline management, communication, risk management, and quality control.

🗓️ Project Timeline (6 Weeks)

Week 1
Client briefing, concept creation, budget estimation, venue shortlisting. Deliverable: Approved concept and budget.
Week 2
Contract signing with venue, host, and photographer. Menu approval. Deliverable: Key contracts closed.
Week 3
Decor ordering, props procurement, invitation design, and dispatch. Deliverable: Invitations sent, decor ordered.
Week 4
Scriptwriting, run-of-show (timing), music playlist coordination, and final purchases. Deliverable: Ready script and timeline.
Week 5
RSVP collection, final vendor confirmations, timeline rehearsal. Deliverable: Final guest list and 100% vendor confirmation.
Week 6
Setup, event execution, teardown, and final reporting. Deliverable: Successful event and post-event report.

Critical Path: Venue Booking → Vendor Contracts (Host/Catering) → Program Finalization → Setup.

⚠️ Risk Management

Risk Probability Impact Mitigation Strategy
Vendor no-show / delay (Host/DJ) Medium High Signed contracts, prepayment, backup vendor shortlist
Budget overrun Medium High 10% reserve fund, weekly budget reviews
Venue mismatch with photos Low High On-site inspection, video tour, specific contract clauses
Low guest attendance Medium Medium RSVP tracking 10 days prior, reminder messages
Technical issues (sound) Medium Medium Equipment test-drive, backup cables/components
Program delays (guests late) High Low 15–20 minute buffer built into the timeline

💡 Key Takeaways (Lessons Learned)

  • The 10% reserve fund is crucial. It is not a formality; it saves the project when a vendor needs replacement or emergency purchases are required.
  • RSVP tracking is vital. Reminders sent 10 days and 2 days before the event significantly increase actual attendance rates.
  • Buffers in the timeline are more important than a "perfect" script. Guests always run late, so a flexible schedule prevents the event from feeling rushed.
  • Always have a backup plan for critical vendors. Having a shortlist of alternative hosts or DJs mitigates the risk of a project failure due to a single person's absence.
